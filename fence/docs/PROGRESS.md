## Stage 6C · Manual and automatic reflection — complete

**Architecture.** `core/reflection.py` verifies the exact persisted source text and completed retain operation for qualifying outcomes, then retrieves relevant historical decisions through the existing provenance-aware recall adapter. It supplies at most the latest 12 qualifying local outcomes and 24 verified source documents to Hindsight reflect. Patterns use a constrained catalog: support capacity, competitor timing, migration path, pilot rollout, and vendor reliability. A pattern requires mentions in at least two distinct proposal/source groups. Hindsight selects themes and source IDs; Fence validates the selection, computes counts, and renders exact source text. Model-written narrative is never displayed as a verified fact. Observed term recurrence is separated from suggested lessons; neither is evidence of causal improvement. Missing or conflicting sources, invalid schemas/citations, and API failures fail safely. Empty/insufficient evidence returns an explicit no-pattern result and avoids the reflect request. Fewer than three patterns is valid.

**Manual UI.** The Patterns section offers **What does our history say?**, loading and completion messages, observed recurrences, suggested lessons, source IDs/dates/text, and limitations. Each explicit click creates a durable job. Normal Streamlit reruns reuse the saved result. Later failures preserve the last successful reflection. Failed jobs expose explicit retry controls. Reflection and job state use the existing durable SQLite path and workspace scope; outcome records are never modified by reflection.

**Automatic trigger contract.** Accepted, Approved, Rejected, Modified, Shipped, and Failed outcome records qualify only after a confirmed Hindsight receipt. Pending decisions, relevance votes, unconfirmed/failed/cancelled writes do not qualify. A failed business outcome is distinct from a failed write. A unique event ledger registers each qualifying event once. In one SQLite transaction, milestones 3, 6, 9, etc. receive unique jobs and immutable outcome snapshots. Atomic tokenized claims allow one active worker per job across sessions/processes sharing the queue. Reruns, duplicate receipt confirmations, and restarts cannot schedule the same milestone twice. The UI executes at most one queued automatic job per rerun; backlogs remain queued. The third successful form save runs its milestone in that same rerun.

**Recovery semantics.** Reflection is bounded by a 90-second engine deadline and existing provider request limits. After 180 seconds, a still-running job is marked interrupted and requires explicit retry; it is never silently resent. Failed jobs also require explicit retry. Claim tokens prevent a late worker from overwriting a newer attempt. Hindsight reflect has no application idempotency key: if a process dies after the remote response but before local persistence, an explicit retry may repeat the remote computation. Exactly-once applies to durable milestone creation and completion ownership, not an impossible guarantee of exactly-once external calls across that uncertainty window. Keep the queue on durable shared storage for the deployed workspace.
